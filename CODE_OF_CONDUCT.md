# Code of Conduct

Treat participants with respect, welcome different experiences, and keep
feedback focused on the work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, and
sharing someone's private information without permission are unacceptable.

Participation follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
including its
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
This applies to the repository, its issues and pull requests, and spaces where
participants represent the project.

## Report privately

Email the Qualcomm GitHub Code of Conduct team at
[github.coc@qti.qualcomm.com](mailto:github.coc@qti.qualcomm.com?subject=GitHub%20Qualcomm%20Code%20of%20Conduct%20Report),
the enforcement contact in the Code of Conduct of
[meta-qcom-distro](https://github.com/qualcomm-linux/meta-qcom-distro). Identify
this repository, describe what happened with relevant links, and say how you
would like to be contacted. Reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the
reporter's privacy is respected.
